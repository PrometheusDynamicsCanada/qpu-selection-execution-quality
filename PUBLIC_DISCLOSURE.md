# Public Disclosure & Intellectual Property Boundary

This repository archives public empirical quantum hardware benchmarks, logical reference circuits/distributions, hardware job identifiers, and standalone verification scripts published by Prometheus Dynamics Canada.

## Withheld Proprietary Methodology
In accordance with Prometheus Dynamics' intellectual property boundary policy across all public repositories:
1. **Compiler & Subgraph Selection:** Internal multi-objective scoring weights, subgraph ranking formulas, routing heuristics, and proprietary synthesis passes are withheld as trade secrets.
2. **Prospective Execution Scheduler:** Internal prospective scheduler feature definitions, fitted regression/risk coefficients, safety margins, and threshold equations are withheld.

## Verifiable Empirical Provenance
All published hardware claims in this repository are independently verifiable from the archived hardware Job IDs / Task ARNs, raw measurement histograms, and standalone verification scripts included in this repository.
